Saga 1

The Legend of the Dragon and the Rise of the Panther

A legendary dragon. A young Marine recruit. A rare Devil Fruit. Follow Akagami as he leaves his old life behind and begins his rise as the Black Panther.

Chapter 1: The World's Most Wanted Man

The world was ruled by the oceans.

Pirates searched for treasure, the Marines fought to maintain order, and powerful individuals battled for the title of Pirate King. Across the seas, one name struck fear into the hearts of kings, admirals, and pirates alike.

RUKAWA — THE AZURE DRAGON.

He was the most wanted pirate in the world, a legendary monster whose adventures had become the stuff of nightmares. His bounty had surpassed that of nearly every pirate in history, and rumors claimed he had fought entire fleets without falling to his knees.

He possessed the legendary Mythical Zoan Devil Fruit, the Uo Uo no Mi, Model: Seiryu, which allowed him to transform into an enormous Azure Dragon.

But while his older brother stood at the peak of the pirate world, his younger brother lived an entirely different life.

AKAGAMI.

A young Marine recruit. Weak in the eyes of the world. Unknown to the great pirates. A nobody wearing the uniform of the very organization hunting his brother.

Yet Akagami had a secret.

He was the younger brother of the most wanted man alive.

And tonight, the two brothers would meet again.

Chapter 2: The Reunion at Their Old Home

The old wooden house stood at the edge of a forgotten village, far away from the busy ports and Marine patrols.

Akagami sat alone inside, wearing his Marine recruit uniform. He stared at the door, waiting for the one person he had not seen in years.

Creaaaak.

The door slowly opened.

A tall figure stepped inside.

He wore a long black coat, heavy boots, and a dark shirt. His hair fell messily around his face, and his sharp eyes carried the weight of countless battles. Scars crossed his body, and the pressure radiating from him made the room feel smaller.

It was Rukawa.

The legendary pirate himself.

Akagami swallowed.

“Big brother...”

Rukawa glanced at him and smirked.

“Look at you. Wearing a Marine uniform now? Never thought I'd see the day.”

Akagami stood up.

“And never thought I'd see you again! Do you have any idea how dangerous it is for you to come here?”

Rukawa laughed.

“Relax. If the Marines knew I was here, this entire island would already be surrounded.”

He sat down, placing his enormous sword against the wall.

For a moment, the two brothers said nothing.

Then Akagami noticed the scars on Rukawa's arms.

“Your adventures... Are the stories true? They say you destroyed a fleet of warships, defeated an admiral, and sailed through the New World without losing a single battle.”

Rukawa leaned back.

“Some are true. Some are exaggerated.”

“Then tell me everything!”

Rukawa grinned.

“Where do I even begin?”

Chapter 3: The Legend of the Azure Dragon

Rukawa began telling his story.

He spoke of islands hidden beyond the Grand Line, monstrous sea creatures, rival pirate crews, and battles against powerful enemies whose names could shake the world.

He had sailed through storms that swallowed entire fleets. He had faced warriors who could split mountains. He had discovered forgotten kingdoms and uncovered secrets the World Government desperately wanted buried.

Akagami listened with wide eyes.

“That's insane! How are you even alive?”

Rukawa laughed.

“Because I'm stronger than most people who try to kill me.”

He raised his hand. A strange blue aura began to surround his body.

“There's another reason, too.”

His body started transforming. Blue scales spread across his arms, sharp claws emerged from his fingers, and powerful horns rose from his head.

Akagami stepped backward.

“Wait...!”

Rukawa's body expanded, filling the room with an overwhelming presence. For a moment, the shadow of a massive dragon stretched across the wooden walls.

“This is the power of the Uo Uo no Mi, Model: Seiryu,” Rukawa explained. “A Mythical Zoan Devil Fruit. It turned me into a dragon.”

Akagami stared in disbelief.

“You really are a monster...”

Rukawa returned to his human form.

“Not a monster. Just someone who refused to let the world decide his limits.”

Akagami clenched his fists.

“Then I'll become strong, too! I'll rise through the Marine ranks, become an admiral, and—”

“An admiral?” Rukawa interrupted.

His smile disappeared.

“Is that really what you want, little brother?”

Akagami fell silent.

Before he could answer, Rukawa reached inside his coat.

“That's why I came here.”

He placed a strange Devil Fruit on the table.

It was dark purple and almost black, covered in mysterious swirling patterns. Its curved shape resembled the head of a wild beast.

Akagami stared at it.

“What... is that?”

Rukawa pushed the fruit toward him.

“Your future.”

Chapter 4: The Gift of the Black Panther

Akagami slowly approached the table.

“Is that a Devil Fruit?”

“Not just any Devil Fruit.”

Rukawa's expression turned serious.

“It's the Neko Neko no Mi, Model: Black Panther. A rare Zoan Devil Fruit that grants its user the powers of a black panther.”

Akagami's jaw dropped.

“YOU'RE GIVING THIS TO ME?!”

“Keep your voice down.”

“But this thing is worth a fortune! And it's a Zoan! How did you even get it?”

Rukawa smirked.

“I have my ways.”

Akagami examined the fruit carefully.

“Why me? Why not give this to one of your crew members? Why not keep it for yourself?”

Rukawa stood up and walked toward the window.

“Because I know you, Akagami. You hate being weak. You hate watching powerful people decide what happens to everyone else.”

He turned toward his younger brother.

“And I know you don't belong behind a desk, following orders from people who have never seen the world beyond their own walls.”

Akagami looked down at his Marine uniform.

Rukawa continued.

“I didn't give you this fruit so you could become my subordinate. I gave it to you because I want you to find your own path.”

Akagami's hands trembled as he picked up the fruit.

“A Mythical Dragon gave me a Devil Fruit...”

He looked at Rukawa.

“You're unbelievable, big brother.”

Rukawa chuckled.

“One warning. Devil Fruits take away your ability to swim. The sea becomes your enemy.”

Akagami grinned.

“Then I'll conquer the sea without swimming!”

Rukawa burst into laughter.

“That's my little brother!”

Without another word, Akagami took a bite.

His face immediately twisted in disgust.

“UGH! THIS TASTES LIKE GARBAGE!”

Rukawa laughed so hard he nearly fell over.

“Congratulations! You've just eaten one of the rarest fruits in the world, and you look like you're about to throw up!”

Akagami swallowed the last piece.

Suddenly, the room went silent.

His pupils narrowed into sharp golden slits.

Black fur spread across his arms. His fingers lengthened into claws, and a dark aura surrounded his body. His muscles tightened as his senses sharpened beyond anything he had ever experienced.

His heart pounded.

He could hear the wind outside.

He could smell the damp earth beneath the house.

He could even hear a mouse moving beneath the floorboards.

Akagami looked at his hands.

“Whoa...”

He raised one arm.

SLASH!

His claws cut through a thick wooden pillar with frightening ease.

Akagami stared at the damage.

“I... I did that?”

Rukawa smiled proudly.

“Welcome to the world of monsters.”

Chapter 5: The Night the Marine Base Fell

Later that night, Akagami stood outside the old house.

His Marine uniform fluttered in the wind.

Rukawa watched him from the doorway.

“Where are you going?”

Akagami turned around.

“To say goodbye.”

“To whom?”

Akagami's eyes narrowed.

“To the Marines.”

Rukawa's grin slowly widened.

“Now that's interesting.”

The Marine base where Akagami had trained stood only a few miles away. Its walls were tall, its soldiers well-armed, and its officers confident that no pirate would dare attack them.

They had spent months treating Akagami as an ordinary recruit.

They had ordered him around, mocked his ambitions, and told him he would never become anything important.

Tonight, he would show them how wrong they were.

Akagami approached the main gate.

“HEY! RECRUIT! YOU'RE OUT PAST CURFEW!”

Two guards blocked his path.

One recognized him.

“Akagami? What are you doing here?”

Akagami smiled.

“I'm resigning.”

The guards exchanged confused looks.

Then one laughed.

“Resigning? You think you can just walk away?”

Akagami lowered his head.

“I'm afraid you misunderstood.”

He raised his eyes.

“I'm not asking permission.”

In an instant, his body blurred.

BOOM!

Both guards were thrown backward as Akagami leaped through the gate.

The alarm began to ring.

“INTRUDER! ALL UNITS, RESPOND!”

Dozens of Marines rushed into the courtyard.

Their commanding officer stepped forward, furious.

“AKAGAMI! HAVE YOU LOST YOUR MIND?!”

Akagami slowly rolled his shoulders.

“Not really. I think I've finally found it.”

The officer drew his sword.

“You're a mere recruit! You think you can challenge an entire Marine base?”

Akagami's grin widened.

“Let's find out.”

His body transformed.

Black fur covered him from head to toe. His shoulders broadened, his claws gleamed beneath the moonlight, and his eyes shone like molten gold.

In seconds, a massive black panther stood where the young recruit had been.

The courtyard fell silent.

One Marine dropped his rifle.

“What... is that?”

Akagami roared.

The sound echoed across the base.

Then he charged.

He moved like a shadow, weaving between soldiers before they could react. His claws struck weapons from their hands, shattered barricades, and tore through the base's defenses. He leaped over walls, landed atop watchtowers, and knocked down groups of Marines with powerful sweeping attacks.

He was fast.

He was ruthless in battle.

And he was only getting started.

The commanding officer charged with a sword raised high.

“YOU TRAITOR!”

Akagami turned, caught the man's weapon between his claws, and snapped it in half.

The officer stared at the broken blade.

Akagami leaned closer.

“Tell headquarters something for me.”

He released the weapon.

“Tell them Akagami is no longer a Marine.”

Then he struck the ground with his claws.

BOOOOM!

A shockwave of shattered stone raced across the courtyard, knocking the remaining soldiers off balance.

The entire base had fallen into chaos.

Akagami stood at the center of it all, his black fur rippling in the wind.

He looked at the Marine flag flying above the headquarters building.

For a moment, he remembered the boy who had once dreamed of wearing that uniform with pride.

Then he turned away.

“Sorry, big brother,” he whispered. “Looks like I'm taking a different road.”

Chapter 6: The Birth of a Pirate

By sunrise, Akagami stood at the harbor.

Behind him, the Marine base was damaged, its officers defeated, and its soldiers scrambling to report the incident.

Akagami removed his Marine cap.

He looked at it for a few seconds before tossing it onto the wooden dock.

Then he unbuttoned his uniform and pulled it off, revealing the dark clothes he had worn beneath it.

He threw the uniform into a nearby barrel.

“Goodbye, Marines.”

A small ship waited at the harbor, its crew watching him approach.

They were not famous pirates. They were outcasts, adventurers, and dreamers who had gathered under Akagami's promise of freedom.

One of them, a tall swordsman named Kaito, stepped forward.

“Captain, are we really doing this?”

Akagami jumped aboard.

“Of course.”

Kaito glanced toward the Marine base.

“You realize they're going to hunt us forever, right?”

Akagami laughed.

“Then they'd better get stronger!”

His crew burst into laughter.

The sails unfurled.

The wind caught them.

And for the first time in his life, Akagami sailed not as a recruit, but as a pirate.

A new flag rose above the ship: a black panther skull with golden eyes, crossed claws beneath it.

His crew cheered.

“LONG LIVE CAPTAIN AKAGAMI!”

Akagami stood at the bow, watching the horizon.

“Next stop?”

Kaito grinned.

“The Grand Line!”

Akagami's eyes gleamed.

“Then let's go find out what this world is hiding!”

The ship sailed toward the open sea.

But far away, inside Marine Headquarters, a report landed on the desk of a high-ranking officer.

The officer read it once.

Then twice.

His expression darkened.

“One recruit... defeated an entire base?”

Another officer stepped forward.

“There's more. Witnesses claim he possessed the abilities of a Black Panther Zoan.”

The room fell silent.

The first officer picked up a photograph of Akagami.

“Find out who gave him that power.”

A second report arrived moments later.

The officer opened it.

His eyes widened.

“Impossible...”

At the top of the page was a name the entire world feared.

RUKAWA — THE AZURE DRAGON.

The officer slowly lowered the paper.

“So the Dragon has a younger brother.”

Chapter 7: A Fifty-Million-Berry Nightmare

Three days later, the first newspaper reached the ports of the East Blue.

Its headline sent shock waves through the pirate world.

MARINE RECRUIT TURNS ROGUE!

Beneath the headline was a photograph of Akagami standing in his black panther form, his golden eyes staring directly at the camera.

The article described the destruction of the Marine base, the mysterious Devil Fruit, and the unknown crew that had escaped with him.

Then came the announcement.

WORLD GOVERNMENT — WANTED

AKAGAMI

DEAD OR ALIVE

฿ 50,000,000

FIFTY MILLION BERRIES

FIRST BOUNTY • THE BLACK PANTHER

Akagami stared at the newspaper aboard his ship.

“FIFTY MILLION?!”

His crew crowded around him.

Kaito whistled.

“Captain, that's a ridiculous first bounty!”

Another crewmate laughed.

“Most pirates spend years trying to reach that amount!”

Akagami scratched his head.

“I thought they'd give me five million at most!”

Kaito folded his arms.

“You attacked a Marine base, defeated its soldiers, stole their peace of mind, and possess a rare Devil Fruit. What did you expect?”

Akagami burst out laughing.

“HAHAHAHA! THIS IS AMAZING!”

He held the newspaper above his head.

“LET THE WORLD KNOW MY NAME!”

His crew roared with him as the ship sailed into the morning sun.

But on a distant island, atop a mountain surrounded by mist, a colossal blue dragon rested among the clouds.

Rukawa opened one golden eye.

A newspaper lay beside him.

He looked at his younger brother's wanted poster and smiled.

“Fifty million already, huh?”

He chuckled.

“Not bad, little brother.”

The dragon lifted his enormous head, staring toward the distant horizon.

“But don't get too comfortable.”

His smile became sharper.

“The sea is cruel. Your Devil Fruit is powerful, but power alone won't make you King of the Pirates.”

Clouds gathered around his horns.

“Show me how far you can go, Akagami.”

The dragon rose into the sky, disappearing into the mist.

And somewhere beyond the horizon, the Black Panther sailed toward his first true adventure.

The world had already learned the name of the Azure Dragon.

Now it would learn the name of his younger brother.

Saga 2

The Black Panther Enters the Grand Line

With a stronger crew, their new ship Marshal, and shipwright John, the Panther Pirates challenge Marine G3 and draw the world’s attention as Akagami’s bounty rises to 250 million berries.

SAGA 2: The Black Panther Enters the Grand Line

Written by: Xian Estoya

Akagami and the Panther Pirates had left the East Blue behind. Their dream was simple: a bigger fortune, a bigger risk, and a bigger reward. With the Grand Line ahead, the crew knew their real adventure was only beginning.

Chapter 1: A Bigger Fortune, A Bigger Risk

The Panther Pirates sailed toward Water Eight, an island famous for its shipwrights. Their old vessel had carried them this far, but Akagami wanted a ship strong enough to survive the Grand Line.

At Water Eight, the crew found a sturdy ship built to endure rough seas. After negotiating with the shipwrights, they bought it and named it Marshal.

“A new ship for a new era,” Akagami declared from the deck. “From now on, we're chasing a bigger fortune, a bigger risk, and a bigger reward!”

The crew cheered. Among them stood Zaito, their skilled swordsman, while Kaito watched the harbor with his usual calm expression.

Chapter 2: The Man with the Robotic Arm

That night, while Akagami slept aboard Marshal, Kaito sat outside in the darkness. A sudden scream echoed through the nearby streets. He followed the sound and found a frightened woman facing a tall, muscular man with an intimidating appearance.

When the man turned toward Kaito, the woman hurried away. Kaito stepped between them and demanded to know what had happened. The man insisted he had done nothing, but their argument quickly grew heated.

The stranger suddenly swung at Kaito. Kaito dodged and prepared to defend himself. His powerful Haki made the stranger realize he had badly underestimated the person in front of him.

Before the conflict could go further, Akagami arrived. The stranger recognized the captain and immediately apologized. He introduced himself as John, a master shipwright with a robotic arm. After a long conversation—and plenty of drinks—John agreed to join the Panther Pirates as their shipwright.

“If you're going to sail into the Grand Line,” John said, “you'll need someone who can keep your ship alive.”

Akagami grinned. “Then welcome aboard!”

Chapter 3: The Assault on Marine G3

At sunrise, Marshal left Water Eight and headed toward Marine G3, a Grade 3 base guarded by towering walls, heavy turrets, and well-trained Marines.

John stepped forward, his robotic arm ready. One by one, the base's turrets were disabled by powerful blasts. Zaito reached the iron gate and sliced through it, opening the way for the Panther Pirates.

Akagami leaped onto the shore and removed his coat, giving his crew the signal. A huge explosion erupted in front of him. He briefly stepped back as smoke swallowed the entrance.

Then the smoke cleared—and a wave of Marines charged out.

“Panther Pirates!” Akagami shouted. “Move!”

The battle exploded across the base. Akagami moved through the fighting with the speed and strength of his Black Panther powers. John held his ground, using his mechanical arm to break through the defenses, while Zaito cut a path through the Marines who tried to stop him.

Chapter 4: Rear Admiral Zodac

Heavy footsteps sounded through the chaos. Akagami turned to face Marine Rear Admiral Zodac, a high-ranking officer determined to stop the pirate captain.

“So you're the Black Panther,” Zodac said. “Your journey ends here.”

Akagami lowered his stance. “People keep telling me that. Nobody's been right yet.”

The two clashed in the middle of the base. Zodac fought fiercely, but Akagami's speed and power pushed him back. By the end of their battle, Zodac was badly injured, while Akagami had only a small cut.

Across the base, John and Zaito defeated their own opponents. With their leaders beaten and their defenses broken, the remaining Marines could no longer hold Marine G3.

The base fell to the Panther Pirates. In the aftermath, three high-ranking Marines were captured and displayed on a wooden cross-shaped frame as a warning. The crew then left Marine G3 behind, while urgent reports raced toward Marine Headquarters.

Chapter 5: The World Takes Notice

Marine Headquarters was shaken by the news. Another base had fallen, and the name Akagami was spreading faster than anyone expected. Officers studied the reports, trying to understand how a rising pirate crew had defeated Marine G3.

A week later, Marshal reached an island controlled by a pirate captain with a bounty of 150 million berries. The rival crew believed their reputation would frighten the newcomers away.

They were wrong.

The Panther Pirates launched their attack. Akagami led from the front, Zaito overwhelmed the enemy's fighters, Kaito held the line, and John used his engineering skills to help the crew push through the island's defenses.

The battle ended with the rival crew defeated, its treasure seized, and the island under the Panther Pirates' control. The rival captain was captured and executed by beheading on Akagami's order. The news spread quickly across the seas.

Chapter 6: A 250-Million-Berry Threat

When the latest report reached Marine Headquarters, the officers were stunned. Akagami had defeated Rear Admiral Zodac, taken Marine G3, and crushed a pirate crew whose captain carried a 150-million-berry bounty.

A new wanted poster was prepared.

WORLD GOVERNMENT — WANTED

AKAGAMI

DEAD OR ALIVE
฿ 250,000,000
TWO HUNDRED FIFTY MILLION BERRIES

CAPTAIN OF THE PANTHER PIRATES

On board Marshal, the crew gathered around the newspaper. Kaito whistled, Zaito gave a satisfied nod, and John laughed at the size of the new bounty.

Akagami looked at the poster, then toward the open sea.

“Two hundred and fifty million, huh?” he said with a grin. “Good. Let the whole world hear our name.”

Marshal's sails filled with wind as the Panther Pirates continued into the Grand Line. Their fortune was growing, the risks were greater, and the world was beginning to fear the Black Panther.

Complete Story • Saga 3

The Rise of the Panthers

This is the complete expanded version of Saga 3, following the Panther Pirates after their victory over the pirate who controlled their previous island. The crew’s names are spreading across the seas—but their next destination will test whether they can become more than feared pirates.

Chapter 1: A Morning of Victory

Morning sunlight spilled across the island the Panther Pirates had conquered. The night before, the crew had celebrated their victory over a captain whose bounty had reached 150 million berries. Now their ship, the Marshal, rested near the shore while the crew gathered around tables crowded with food and treasure.

Akagami sat among his crewmates, listening to their laughter. Kaito was already boasting about the next fight, while John inspected the damage the ship had suffered. Their victory had brought them wealth, but Akagami knew the world would soon answer their actions. A crew that attacked Marine bases and toppled powerful pirates could not remain unknown for long.

A flock of News Coos descended over the island, carrying fresh newspapers. The crew’s celebration quieted as the first wanted posters were unfolded. Their names had become news across the seas.

Chapter 2: The New Bounties

Akagami’s new poster showed a bounty of 250,000,000 berries. Kaito’s bounty had climbed to 100,000,000 berries, while John’s was set at 50,000,000 berries. The crew stared at the numbers, realizing that the Government no longer saw them as a small band of troublemakers.

Across the sea, bounty hunters began searching for the Panther Pirates, hoping to claim the rewards. Other pirates watched the news with interest, wondering whether Akagami’s crew was a rising power—or a target they could eliminate before it grew stronger. The first hunters who tried to corner them learned that the new bounties came with a dangerous warning.

Far away, a mysterious high-ranking Marine read the report. The official’s expression hardened at Akagami’s name. The Panther Pirates were becoming a problem, and the Marine intended to learn how serious that problem might become.

Chapter 3: The Kingdom Under Strings

The Marshal sailed toward Rose Kingdom, a once-prosperous country known for its beautiful gardens, tall towers, and grand palace. But when the Panther Pirates approached its coast, they saw no joyful crowds or welcoming harbor. The streets looked broken, the fields were neglected, and frightened citizens moved beneath the watch of armed men.

Rose Kingdom had fallen under the rule of Toffy, a tall, slender pirate who possessed the String-String Fruit, the Ito Ito no Mi. Toffy and his crew had defeated the kingdom’s army, humiliated its king, and forced the people to live under their control. His strings were more than a weapon: they had become a symbol of how tightly he held the country in his grasp.

Akagami watched a family being driven from their home to make room for Toffy’s men. He saw people who had been robbed of their safety and dignity. The Panther Pirates had come seeking supplies, but the suffering around them changed their purpose. Akagami decided they would challenge Toffy and free Rose Kingdom.

Chapter 4: The March on the Palace

The crew spent days preparing. John checked weapons and reinforced equipment, Kaito sharpened his blade, and Akagami studied the palace and the routes leading to it. The Panther Pirates gathered allies and divided their forces, knowing that Toffy’s crew would not surrender its power without a fight.

At last, dozens of Panthers marched toward the palace gates. Their advance shattered the uneasy silence of the capital. The entrance was wrecked, and the first clash sent smoke rising over the rooftops. From the top of a nearby house, Akagami, John, and Kaito watched the battle unfold, waiting for a clear sign of the enemy’s true strength.

As the smoke spread, the Panthers’ advance slowed. Fighters began falling back, and some did not rise again. When the haze finally cleared, an unknown attacker’s handiwork lay across the ruined entrance. Then figures appeared high in the sky—hundreds of Toffy’s men, suspended and moving in formation. The enemy had been waiting above them.

Chapter 5: Three Days of War

The battle for Rose Kingdom became a struggle across streets, rooftops, and palace grounds. The Panther Pirates fought Toffy’s forces while Akagami, Kaito, and John pushed toward the strongest fighters protecting the palace. Every path forward was contested, and the crew had to rely on teamwork to keep from being surrounded.

Day turned to night, then back to day. The fighting continued for three days. The Panthers took losses, but they refused to abandon the citizens who had placed their hopes in them. Kaito’s sword opened paths through the enemy lines, while John used his strength and robotic arm to break through defenses. Akagami kept moving toward the palace, determined to confront the man responsible for the kingdom’s misery.

By the third day, the Mingo Pirates’ formation began to collapse. Their strongest fighters had been pushed back, and the Panthers gained ground. But the battle was not over. Toffy had yet to show himself—and the crew was about to learn why his name had ruled Rose Kingdom for so long.

Chapter 6: The String-String Devil

Toffy finally appeared above the battlefield, floating in the air as if the ground itself were beneath his notice. Thin strings glimmered around him, almost invisible against the smoke and dust. With a flick of his hand, he sent them snapping through the battlefield, forcing fighters to scatter and breaking the Panthers’ advance.

John and Kaito rushed to challenge him, but Toffy’s control of the battlefield overwhelmed them. He used his strings to redirect their attacks and strike from angles they could barely anticipate. Both were badly beaten and left struggling to stand. Toffy descended toward Kaito, preparing to finish the swordsman while the remaining Panthers fought desperately below.

Akagami saw his crewmate in danger. Something powerful surged inside him—an overwhelming will that burst outward in a wave of red pressure. The air seemed to tremble. Fighters across the battlefield lost their nerve, and Toffy himself dropped to his knees under the force of Akagami’s Conqueror’s Haki.

Toffy looked up, stunned. “That kind of Haki belongs to a true king,” he said. Akagami stepped forward. “Then you should never have used your power to make these people kneel.”

Chapter 7: The Black Panther vs. Toffy

The two captains clashed, and the battlefield seemed to shrink around them. Toffy attacked with strings that cut through stone and controlled the space between them. Akagami answered with his speed, strength, and unbreakable resolve, forcing his way through attacks that would have stopped an ordinary fighter.

The fight lasted for hours. Toffy tried to keep Akagami at a distance, turning the ground and ruined buildings into traps. Akagami adapted, watching for openings and pressing forward whenever Toffy’s control slipped. Neither captain was willing to yield. Every exchange carried the weight of the kingdom’s future.

At last, Akagami found his opening and landed the decisive blow. He stood battered, with a few cuts and bruises, but still on his feet. Toffy lay defeated, his grip on Rose Kingdom broken. The surviving Mingo Pirates could no longer hold their formation, and the people began to emerge from hiding as the fighting ended.

Chapter 8: A Kingdom Reborn

With Toffy defeated, the Panther Pirates turned their attention to the kingdom itself. They helped clear the damaged streets, secured the palace, and stopped the remaining forces from terrorizing the people. Akagami refused to claim the throne. Instead, he arranged for the old king to return and rule again, with the Panther Pirates promising to protect Rose Kingdom from those who would enslave it once more.

The kingdom’s recovery would take time. Buildings had to be repaired, farms restored, and families reunited. Yet for the first time in years, citizens could gather without fearing Toffy’s men. The old king thanked Akagami, but Akagami made it clear that the people had to be the ones to rebuild their home.

Before the crew departed, Rose Kingdom celebrated its freedom. For some, the Panthers were still frightening pirates. For others, they had become the force that gave their country a second chance. Akagami accepted that contradiction. He had not come to be called a hero—but he would not ignore people who needed help.

Chapter 9: The Doctor Named Blitz

Among those who approached the crew after the battle was Blitz, a skilled doctor with a calm voice and sharp eyes. His Devil Fruit, the Heal Heal Fruit, allowed him to heal injuries quickly, but each use consumed his own energy. He had spent the battle tending to wounded citizens and Panthers, pushing himself until he could barely remain standing.

Akagami recognized the value of a doctor who risked himself to save others. He offered Blitz a place aboard the Marshal. Blitz agreed, provided the crew understood that his power had limits: he could accelerate healing, but he could not spend his energy carelessly without putting himself in danger.

With Blitz’s help, the injured crew received treatment and began recovering. The Panther Pirates held a celebration to welcome their newest member and honor the people of Rose Kingdom. For the first time since arriving, the crew could rest without the sound of battle around them.

Chapter 10: The World Shakes Again

News of Toffy’s defeat reached Marine Headquarters. Reports described the fall of the Mingo Pirates, the liberation of Rose Kingdom, and Akagami’s sudden display of Conqueror’s Haki. The Government understood that the Panther Pirates had crossed another threshold. They were no longer simply raiders attacking isolated targets; their captain had defeated a powerful ruler and changed the balance of a kingdom.

The new wanted posters soon followed. Akagami’s bounty rose to 600,000,000 berries. Kaito’s climbed to 300,000,000 berries, and John’s rose to 150,000,000 berries. The crew studied the numbers aboard the Marshal, aware that the rewards would attract even more dangerous opponents.

Across the seas, the updated posters caused fresh alarm. Some pirates wanted to test Akagami’s strength, others hoped to profit from the bounties, and the Marines began treating the Panthers as a growing threat. Their victory had freed a kingdom—but it had also made the world pay closer attention.

Chapter 11: The Dragon’s Pride

Far away, a News Coo delivered Akagami’s latest bounty to Rukawa, captain of the Spike Pirates. The Azure Dragon studied his younger brother’s poster, noting the jump to 600 million berries. A proud smile crossed his face. Akagami had chosen his own path, and he was growing stronger without hiding behind his older brother’s reputation.

Rukawa’s own wanted poster revealed a staggering bounty of 8,800,000,000 berries. The number reflected the fear and history surrounding the legendary Azure Dragon. Yet as he looked at Akagami’s picture, it was not his own fame that mattered. He was proud that his brother was becoming a name the world could no longer ignore.

The two brothers remained on separate seas, leading different crews toward different futures. But the connection between them endured. Rukawa knew that one day the world might force their paths together—and when that day came, neither brother would be the same person he had been at the beginning of their journey.

Chapter 12: The Supreme Marine’s Fury

The report reached the mysterious supreme high-ranking Marine who had been watching Akagami’s rise. The official read the new bounty figures, then stopped at Rukawa’s name and the enormous sum attached to it. The connection between the Azure Dragon and the Black Panther made the situation more dangerous than the Marine had first believed.

Anger hardened into resolve. The Panther Pirates were growing, and the Spike Pirates were led by one of the most feared figures on the seas. The Marine would not allow the brothers’ influence to expand unchecked. Orders were prepared, intelligence gathered, and the next move began taking shape in the shadows.

For now, the Marine kept their identity and plans hidden from the public. But the world’s response had begun. The brothers’ names were now linked in the minds of powerful people—and that attention would follow them into the next stage of their voyage.

Chapter 13: Sailing Toward the New World

Back aboard the Marshal, John finished repairing the ship while the crew prepared to leave Rose Kingdom. Blitz checked the last of the injured, Kaito inspected his sword, and Akagami stood at the bow, looking toward the open sea. The kingdom was safe for now, and the crew had a new doctor, higher bounties, and a reputation that had spread far beyond the island.

The Panthers gathered for one final celebration before departure. They had lost comrades and endured three days of war, but they had also freed a kingdom and proven they could stand against a powerful enemy. Akagami knew that the New World would bring stronger opponents and harder choices. He welcomed the challenge.

As the Marshal pulled away from the shore, Rose Kingdom’s people watched from the harbor. Akagami did not promise that he would return, but he left them under the protection of their restored king. The sails filled, the sea opened before them, and the Panther Pirates set course for the New World—where their next legend would begin.
